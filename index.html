Long-horizon manipulation on the SO-101 with Q-chunking

Joint work with Prajwal Avhad

I’m extending Squint, a fast visual RL method for sim-to-real on the SO-101 arm, to multi-stage tasks like stacking, unstacking and packing several cubes, which plain RL from scratch doesn’t solve.

  • Built new long-horizon tasks (unstacking, towers, tray packing, rearranging) with scripted motion-planning solvers that record demonstrations in the policy’s own observation and action format.
  • Train a flow-matching policy that outputs chunks of 5 actions (QC-FQL), pretrained offline on demos and then improved with online RL, from only a 16×16 wrist-camera image and joint state so it can transfer to the real arm.
  • Recovery demos with noise and deliberate missed grasps, plus a pessimistic critic target, took 3-cube unstacking to 91% success; lifting reaches 94% with domain randomization for real-robot deployment.
